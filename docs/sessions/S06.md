- [x] Extra (user's pick once the goal was done): PNG download per panel (R45)

## Log
**Decisions** (user): see [ADR 0006](../decisions/0006-product-characteristics-and-png-export.md).
- Goal order: R17 + R18 before line clears.
- Characteristics are **labels only**: each product keeps its own demand and capabilities.
- The name defaults to `X-Y-Z` and can be overridden. Clearing it goes back to the default.
- Demo X = K, L, M, N, so names don't clash with sites A and B.
- Headers in the demand and plan views show names, not P-ids.
- The goal was done in ~9 min. For the rest of the session the user picked R45 (PNG download).

**Built**
- Model:
  - `Dataset.characteristics` and `Product.variants`;
  - `productName` / `combinationName` / `firstFreeCombination` in `src/model/products.ts`;
  - reducer actions for renaming characteristics and variants, and adding or removing variants
    (max 8; a variant in use can't be removed);
  - validation for duplicate combinations and names and for missing variants;
  - files from before S06 get the demo characteristics and a combination per product, and keep
    their names.
- Demo: X K–N, Y 1–3, Z six trees (72 combinations); 20 products, 45 capabilities.
  - Total demand is ~42 M units. The estimated machine load is 76–91 %.
  - The LP solves to optimal with no unmet demand (~120 ms in dev).
- UI:
  - a **Characteristics** tab (three cards, usage counts);
  - Products table: X/Y/Z selects, with the default name as the placeholder;
  - every view shows names (nowrap headers);
  - a 20-colour product palette.
- R45:
  - a shared `Panel` (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`) used by all 3 Overview, 3 Demand and 3 Plan panels;
  - `html-to-image` at 2×, with `.light-theme` and `.png-export` applied while rendering;
  - controls are left out of the image.

**Tested**
- Unit: naming, renames carrying through, the variant limit and removal guard, first free
  combination, validation messages, pre-S06 migration, and the seed shape.
- e2e:
  - a characteristics flow: rename a variant; a duplicate is flagged; a custom name shows on the
    Overview, and clearing it reverts;
  - export: in dark mode at 900 px, the PNG is ≥ 2× the full table width with a light, opaque
    background; every page has its download buttons.
  - Demand and overview expectations are updated for the new demo.
- Looked at the exported PNGs (shifts table, network map, weekly chart) and the new tabs in the
  browser pane.

## Outcome
Goal met, plus R45. `npm test` is green: 5 tooling tests, typecheck, 66 unit tests and 22 e2e
tests. With 20 products the per-product tables now scroll sideways on narrow screens. That is R44
(grouping), which is still open.
