## Log
- **User decision:** skip the deploy, keep the app local for now.
- **Built:**
  - Model ([src/model](../../src/model)): `Dataset` with sites, storage locations, truck lanes,
    machines, products, capabilities and settings.
  - `effectiveRate`, `lotOutput` (the line clear eats into the shift), `unitsPerPallet` and
    `availableShifts` (calendar − holidays − maintenance).
  - `validateDataset` returns readable errors: references, duplicates, OEE range, products with
    no capable machine, invalid dates, line clear vs shift length.
- **Seed:** P01–P03 B only, P04–P06 both sites, P07–P10 A only. Storage: B-WH 400, A-IF 250 and
  A-WH 600 pallets. Trucks: 5/wk × 30 pallets, no weekends. Max campaign: 21 shifts. Holidays and
  maintenance dates are in 2027.
- **Overview:**
  - stat tiles;
  - React Flow map: machines → local storage, a B-WH → A-WH truck edge, and A stores → next
    process step;
  - machine cards per site with effective-rate bars and available shifts in 2027;
  - product table: hovering a product dims machines that can't make it.
- **Technical defaults** (no ADR needed):
  - design tokens as CSS variables mapped into Tailwind v4 `@theme`, light/dark via
    `prefers-color-scheme`;
  - Tableau 10 palette for products;
  - OEE stored as percent, line clears in minutes;
  - shadcn/ui and ECharts not added yet; they come in when needed.
- **Tested:**
  - unit: 13 tests (seed integrity, validation, capacity maths);
  - Playwright: 5 tests (sites/machines, map nodes and truck label, hover highlight, light/dark
    screenshots);
  - manual check in the browser pane at desktop and 375 px: no horizontal scroll; the map needed
    `minZoom` lowered to fit on a phone.

## Outcome
Goal met, without the deploy (deferred by the user). `npm test` is green: 5 tooling tests,
typecheck, 13 unit tests and 5 e2e tests.
