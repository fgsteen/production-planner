- **Baseline at 2× demand:** P07, P10, P11, P13 and P17 100 % short, P01 62 %, P20 20 %; 24 % of
  demand unmet in total.
- **Model** ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-fair-share-unmet-demand.md)): the worst share F, plus 10
  slices of each product's share that cost twice the last. A first try had F too weak (10 × SHORT):
  with a 4× rate gap the slices won (P 50 %, Q 62.5 %). F now outweighs the dearest slice. A probe
  with two overloaded machines: the worst level exact (60/60), the other approximate (10/22.5
  against a fair 20/20).
- **Result at 2×:** every product 33–34 % short, but 36 % of demand unmet in total (24 % before).
- **Time budgets:** the fix stage took 25 s at 2× with the new costs. Skipping optional LP solves
  after 40 % / 60 % of the time limit brings it to 10.1 s at 2× (31.6 s), 6.4 s at 1.3× (11.9 s),
  and 6.0 s at 1×. Line clears are about the same.
- **UI:** an "Unmet demand" panel (share bar, units, 52-week strip) and fully used machines in red
  in the shifts table. Checked in the browser at 2× demand (all six machines at 100 %), e2e extended.
- **R66:** validation error per capability. Two tests that switch the demand site to B now expect it.
